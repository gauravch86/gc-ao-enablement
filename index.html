Across Telefonica Peru, Chile and Argentina, and continuing into H3G Ireland and H3G UK, the operating model started with establishing the right observability — monitoring that captures what is going wrong in production. With volume and categorization visible, we ran top-offender analysis to isolate root causes: code, operational gaps, data problems, or user/business constraints — then drove permanent closure by impact volume.

Containment followed an SRE / governance model (Git-versioned workarounds, controlled deploy, impact identification) so known issues could not hit new customers. In parallel with Dev, root-cause fixes were prioritized and workarounds sunset in a controlled manner. At H3G UK this scaled into self-healing frameworks, GenAI SOP enablement, SPOG (Grafana) business-domain monitoring, and an operating model that cut 474 → 336 FTEs while improving P1/outage outcomes — autonomy-centric KPIs in practice: incident avoidance, efficiency, scalability.

H3G UK

$125m MGS — Vendor Consolidation deal FY23–24

Problem

  • Challenges with Availability & Stability — 3UK platform had a high volume of Outages/P1s impacting NPS continuously across FY21–22–23
  • Very tight budget (OPEX cut by 20%) to build the required team as the deal came with many unknowns and vectors beyond control — sunset of ~15% of apps, missing SOPs, poorly maintained infra, back-and-forth scope ambiguity among vendors, etc.
  • 4 major vendors across the estate: TCS — L2/L3 for IT/Infra/Cloud/50+ apps; TechM — L1/SD/NSD for full H3G scope and L2/L3 for 300+ apps; NTT — L2/L3 for Data/BI (15–20 apps); THIS (Torry Harris) — SOA / TIBCO middleware (30+ app components)
  • In summary: 400+ major app components in Managed Services scope — won with an up-front 20% discount, replacing 4 vendors via handover, SOP documentation, and a hard rule of zero transition-caused outages
  • Transition planned in 4 phases: KT → Forward Shadow → Reverse Shadow → Need-basis engagement

Steps taken

  • Led the deal with pre-sales: scoped the estate, analyzed 1 year of incidents per application component, established 75 CPI/KPIs, built a strict 6-month transition plan, and modeled P&L from a starting team of 336 FTEs replacing 474 FTEs across 4 vendors — including ~15% application-scope sunset plus AI/ML, automation, SOP and efficiency levers
  • Deal signed after an 8-month marathon with CIO, CTO and Operations Director; triggered Jan 2024 → Jun 2024
  • Hired 36 FTEs in UK and 300 in India; Security and EUC outsourced to 2 vendors while leadership for both scopes stayed under my ownership
  • Integrated existing Dynatrace & Splunk into SPOG (Single Pane of Glass on Grafana) for business-domain monitoring alerts — delivered to 24×7 Service Delivery Managers for immediate business-impact visibility
  • Closed major knowledge gaps: SOPs built with Generative AI from KT recordings and manual notes into a usable knowledge repository
  • 30–40% of servers had last startup/maintenance older than 1 year — scheduled and remediated; directly linked to prior-semester P1s/outages
  • Self-healing frameworks for hard-failure components (app/platform constraints); projects triggered for modernization and right-sized infra
  • Predict / Prevent / Restore became seamless — lowest P1/outage levels in 2024–2025 despite a landmark transition: ~15% apps sunset, 474 → 336 FTEs, modernization, automation, and a new 24×7 operating lens

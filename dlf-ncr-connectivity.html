Citizen story

One morning, one office — two completely different Delhis

Persona: a professional living in Gurugram (Sector 49 / SPR belt) who must reach an office in Ghaziabad (Hindon / Raj Nagar Extension side) by 9:30 AM. Distance is only ~55–80 km — but today’s route is forced through Delhi’s peak choke points.

Today — through the core

Painful peak

Typical morning drive via NH-48 → Mahipalpur / Dhaula Kuan → Ring Road → NH-24

2.5 – 3.5 hrs

Leave home by ~6:00–6:15 AM for a 9:30 start — or gamble and arrive late.

  • NH-48 / Mahipalpur merge jams before the airport even appears
  • Dhaula Kuan and Ring Road absorb VIP / high-security movement freezes
  • Stop-start fuel burn, unpredictable ETA, high cognitive load
  • Freight and passenger traffic share the same inner corridors
  • Any incident near central Delhi cascades across the whole NCR

Sources (illustrative ranges): Uber Intercity ~83 km / ~3 hrs average; public guides 1.5–2.5 hrs off-peak and 2–3+ hrs peak via NH-48 + NH-9; Rome2Rio ~56 km free-flow ~53 min (rarely available at office hour).

After network completion — western / northern bypass

Designed outcome

Dwarka Expy (#1) → UER-II Phase-I (#9A) → UER-II Ext (#10B/#10C) → Ghaziabad

75 – 95 min

Leave ~7:45–8:00 AM. Stay west and north of the restricted central zone.

  • Signal-free Dwarka Expressway already operational (Kherki Daula → Shiv Murti)
  • UER-II acts as Delhi’s third ring — skirts Najafgarh, Rohini, Alipur
  • Extension Link (#10B/#10C) is the missing bridge into Ghaziabad–Noida–FBD
  • High-security / ministerial precincts are never on the path
  • More predictable door-to-desk time; lower fuel and stress

Grounding: UER-II + Dwarka Expy inauguration reporting cites Singhu/airport cuts from ~2 hrs → ~20–40 min on western arcs; Delhi–Gurugram target ~30 min. Full GGN→GZB benefit depends on completing Extension Link Parts II–III (consultant being finalised on the Oct 2026 tracker).

Useful intelligence

What DLF can put on the table for NHAI

Independent reading of the Oct 2026 tracker: celebrate what is open, sequence what still blocks the citizen outcome, and keep freight off the high-security core.

01

Close the Ghaziabad hinge first

#1 and #9A are operational. The citizen GGN→GZB story still waits on #10B/#10C (consultant being finalised). Prioritise that hinge for the northern bypass promise.

02

Keep two escape valves

Northern UER-II ring for west→east office trips; southern #18 DND–FBD–KMP for SPR / Faridabad-origin traffic. Resilience matters when one arc is under maintenance.

03

Protect the restricted core by design

Messaging for ministers: this network is not “more cars in Lutyens” — it is a deliberate outer ring so VIP / high-security precincts stay quieter.

04

Fix the 1.6 km Delhi bottleneck

Upper Dwarka Expy (#12) is complete in Gurugram but still notes a 1.6 km Delhi-side bottleneck — a high-ROI micro-fix before mega packages.

05

SPR + Greater SPR are Gurugram’s south gate

#2 tenders and #5 land acquisition decide whether southern Gurugram feeds cleanly into #16/#18 instead of dumping onto NH-48.

06

Measure what citizens feel

Suggest NHAI publish a simple morning dashboard: GGN→GZB ETA via core vs ring, incident minutes, and freight share on UER-II — so outcomes stay visible.

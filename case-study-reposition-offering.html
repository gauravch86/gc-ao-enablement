Strategic Product Manager · Case Study 1

Reposition an underperforming services offering
in the portfolio

A world-class telecom vendor holds Application Managed Services (AMS) in the portfolio — strong technical delivery and customer satisfaction on live accounts, but flat net sales and declining gross margin over the last two years. Global Sales cannot articulate differentiated value vs. SI competitors; pricing is inconsistent across regions and Deal Desk approvals are slow on complex bids. Leadership asks: evolve, reposition, or sunset — and wants a plan.

Net sales (2-yr trend)
Flat
Pipeline conversion weak · no attach growth
Gross margin
−4–6 pts
Discounting · scope creep · delivery cost drift
Delivery NPS / C-SAT
Strong
Technical execution is not the problem
Working hypothesis: The offering is commercially mis-positioned — sold as FTE headcount and effort hours while the market buys outcomes, autonomy, and TCO reduction. Delivery excellence exists; the product story, packaging, and pricing model do not.
Diagnose before deciding Win/loss · margin · competitive · voice-of-customer Reposition + evolve (not sunset) Guardrails · enablement · feedback loop

Credibility lens: Same commercial muscles used in H3G UK ($125m vendor consolidation — packaging, discount vs margin, 474→336 economics) and TEF Argentina ($100m renewal — reposition accountability & KPI transparency when the story was broken).

Deliverable 1 · Diagnosis framework

What data to gather — five lenses before any portfolio call

I would run a 4-week commercial diagnostic (parallel workstreams, one steering forum) before recommending evolve / reposition / sunset. Each lens has an owner, a data source, and a decision trigger — so we separate delivery quality from commercial failure modes.

Lens A

Win / loss & pipeline quality

  • Inputs: 24-month CRM pipeline; stage conversion; bid/no-bid; competitive set per deal
  • Win/loss interviews: 15–20 deals (won + lost) — buyer, procurement, technical evaluator
  • Segment: by region, deal size, tower (App / Infra / Network), greenfield vs renewal
  • Trigger: if loss reason = “price only” >40% → packaging/pricing broken; if “value unclear” >30% → positioning broken
Lens B

Margin & deal economics

  • Inputs: P&L by account × offering; GM% trend; transition cost actuals vs bid
  • Discount waterfall: list → approved → signed; regional variance; Deal Desk override rate
  • Scope creep: change-request volume; unpaid extras; FTE actuals vs contract
  • Trigger: if GM erosion >50% from discount (not delivery) → guardrails gap; if transition overruns systemic → packaging underestimates KT
Lens C

Competitive & market shift

  • Inputs: analyst views (Gartner/IDC); competitor rate cards; AO / AIOps positioning
  • Map: who wins on “FTE arbitrage” vs “outcome / autonomy” vs “product-led MS”
  • Attach: Ericsson product pull-through on AMS wins vs losses
  • Trigger: if market moving to outcome-tier pricing and we still quote pure FTE → reposition required
Lens D

Customer & delivery voice

  • Inputs: NPS / C-SAT / E-SAT by account; QBR themes; escalation logs
  • Sales feedback: structured survey — “what do you wish you could sell?”
  • Delivery: what customers praise vs what they refuse to pay premium for
  • Trigger: high delivery score + low willingness to renew at rate → value prop mismatch, not execution failure
Lens E · Portfolio fit

Strategic adjacency & sunset criteria

  • Inputs: roadmap overlap with AO / cloud / product MS; cannibalization risk; sunk tooling cost
  • Revenue recognition / Finance: contract structures that block outcome pricing today
  • Sunset test: no path to GM ≥ target within 18 months AND no strategic pull-through → sunset candidate
  • Reposition test: strong delivery + attach potential + fixable commercial model → reposition candidate

Diagnostic scorecard (how I synthesize the call)

Signal Healthy Amber Red → action
Pipeline conversion >25% qualified→won 15–25% <15% → fix value prop + sales enablement
GM% vs portfolio target Within 2 pts 2–5 pts below >5 pts → pricing/guardrails or scope model broken

Diagnosis · Expected findings (illustrative)

What the data would likely show — root causes, not symptoms

Based on the scenario inputs and patterns from large MGS portfolios, I would expect three reinforcing root causes — all commercial, none requiring a delivery turnaround.

Root cause 1

Commoditized packaging

  • Offer = FTE towers (L1/L2/L3) with weak linkage to business KPIs
  • Sales defaults to rate-per-FTE — easy for procurement to bench against TCS/HCL/IBM
  • No modular “ramp” — customer must buy the whole pyramid or nothing
  • Symptom: flat sales; long cycles; technical wins that die in procurement
Root cause 2

Pricing chaos & margin leakage

  • Regions discount independently to hit quota — no global floor
  • Transition / KT underestimated in bid → margin bleeds in Y1–Y2
  • Scope creep absorbed to protect NPS — unpaid work erodes GM
  • Symptom: −4–6 pt GM decline while delivery scores hold
Root cause 3

Value story gap vs market

  • Buyers want TCO reduction, autonomy path, single accountability
  • We pitch headcount replacement and tool-agnostic runbooks
  • AO / observability IP exists elsewhere in portfolio but not attached to AMS SKU
  • Symptom: Sales “cannot explain value”; losses on narrative, not capability
Implication: Sunset would destroy a delivery-capable franchise and Ericsson account footprint. Pure “evolve” (incremental tooling) without repositioning the commercial SKU will not fix flat sales or margin. The play is reposition the offering and evolve the product modules underneath.
Deliverable 2 · Recommendation & trade-offs

Reposition — with selective evolve; do not sunset

Recommendation: REPOSITION Application Managed Services from “FTE-based application support” to Outcome-Tiered Application Operations — the commercial entry ramp into the Autonomous Operations journey (feeds the broader AO portfolio; see Case Study 2).

Sunset

Rejects strong delivery DNA, installed base, and Ericsson pull-through. High exit cost; competitors capture footprint. Only if Lens E shows no 18-month GM path — not met here.

Evolve only

Tooling refresh without SKU/pricing change fixes delivery incrementally but not sales narrative or margin guardrails. Necessary but insufficient alone.

Reposition ✓

Reframe what we sell (outcome tiers + modular packs), how we price (CPI bands + platform fee), and how Sales tells the story (TCO → autonomy path). Evolve modules underneath.

What reposition means

From → To

  • From: “We provide X FTEs for application support”
  • To: “We guarantee application availability & MTTR tiers — with a defined path to observability, predictive ops, and autonomy”
  • Three commercial tiers: Stabilize · Optimize · Autonomize
  • AMS becomes the land; AO modules become the expand
Trade-offs accepted

What we give up / risks

  • Short-term revenue: smaller initial deals (tier entry vs full FTE pyramid) — compensated by attach & renewal uplift
  • Sales retraining: 1–2 quarter productivity dip during enablement
  • Delivery refactor: standardize KPI catalogues — upfront product/GTM cost
  • Risk mitigated: sunset avoids these costs but surrenders market; evolve-only prolongs margin bleed
Updated value proposition (one line): “Ericsson Application Operations — outcome-guaranteed managed applications with a built-in ramp from stabilization to autonomous operations, reducing customer TCO and governance load while protecting margin through standardized tiers and global pricing guardrails.”
Deliverable 3 · Pricing, packaging & value proposition

Revised commercial model — tiers, not towers

Packaging shifts from headcount blocks to outcome tiers + modular capability packs. FTE sizing stays internal (for delivery planning) — customer buys service classes & CPI attainment.

Tier Customer outcome What's included Pricing model Typical buyer
Stabilize Restore control — backlog burn, SLA baseline, single accountability L1–L3 app ops · ITSM integration · monthly CPI board · top-offender program Platform fee + per-app-class unit · floor GM 22% CIO / Head of Apps — “stop the bleeding”
Optimize −15–20% ops TCO · faster MTTR · proactive problem ratio Stabilize + unified observability · SPOG · GenAI SOP · automation factory Outcome band pricing (Avail / MTTR tiers) + gain-share on TCO proof COO / VP Ops — “prove TCO before full AO”
Autonomize Path to L3–L4 autonomy · self-heal on policy-approved classes Optimize + closed-loop agents · policy packs · reuse library · soft-ramp CPI contract Milestone + performance (M3/M12/M18 gates) · links to Case Study 2 AO SKU CTO / Chief Digital — “autonomy as product”

Modular attach packs

  • Observability jump-start — 90-day SPOG stand-up
  • Top-offender sprint — 6-month RC backlog program
  • Transition accelerator — KT/shadow factory (priced, not free)
  • Vendor consolidation bridge — pre-AO operating-model unlock

Pricing mechanics

  • Global rate card — list price by tier × app criticality class
  • Regional index — ±15% band (not ±40% ad hoc)
  • Transition — fixed-fee KT module; no margin bleed in Y1
  • Renewal uplift — tier step-up clause when CPI gates met

What Sales can finally say

  • “Buy stabilization now — not a 5-year FTE gamble”
  • “TCO reduction is contractual, not a slide promise”
  • “One throat to choke from app ops → autonomy”
  • “Ericsson product + 3PP under one MS operating model”

H3G UK parallel: Won by scoping 400+ components, 75 CPI/KPIs, and a 6-month transition plan — not by being cheapest per FTE. Repositioned the bid as operating-model transformation with priced economics (474→336), not staff augmentation.

Deliverable 3 (cont.) · Commercial guardrails & Deal Desk

Pricing guidelines for Sales & Deal Desk on complex deals

Guardrails turn regional chaos into predictable margin — Deal Desk becomes a deal architect, not a discount gate.

Guardrail Rule Deal Desk action if breached
Floor GM% Stabilize ≥22% · Optimize ≥26% · Autonomize ≥30% (post-transition) Auto-escalate to Regional Product Head; require scope reduction or tier downgrade
Max discount ≤12% off list without EVP approval; ≤8% for renewals Discount must be traded for scope, term, or tier step-up at M12
Transition pricing KT module mandatory on all new logos; min 8% of Y1 ACV No exceptions — underpriced KT is #1 margin killer in diagnostic
Scope change Out-of-scope CR within 5 business days; unpaid work cap 2% ACV SDM + Product Owner sign-off; feeds account P&L review
Regional variance Like-for-like bid within ±15% of global rate card index >15% requires written competitive intel + win-strategy approval
CPI / penalty Soft-ramp months 1–6; credit caps aligned to tier; penalty parking lot in P&L Finance sign-off on rev-rec treatment before signature

Deal Desk toolkit (enablement)

  • ROI calculator — TCO baseline vs tier outcome (customer-facing)
  • Margin waterfall — internal bid model (FTE + tools + transition + risk)
  • Tier selector — discovery questionnaire → recommended SKU
  • Competitive battlecards — vs FTE arbitrage SIs; vs cloud-native MSPs
  • Executive one-pager per tier — “what buyer gets in 90 days”

Complex deal playbook

  • Multi-tower: App Ops land → Network/Infra expand — separate SOWs, one governance
  • Consolidation bid: mandate vendor-reduction narrative; price governance −20% as customer ROI
  • Renewal at risk: tier step-up or modular attach — never pure rate cut
  • Partner / 3PP: thin-adapter pricing; Ericsson MS umbrella owns CPI outcome
Deliverable 4 · Investment prioritization

Sequence for ROI — what to fund, defer, and stop

Repositioning fails if we fund everything at once. I would sequence commercial assets first (they unblock sales this quarter), then reusable delivery IP (margin next year), and explicitly deprioritize low-ROI work.

Q1 · 0–90 days
Sell differently

Rate card · tier definitions · ROI calc · battlecards · Deal Desk rules live

Q2 · 3–6 months
Deliver to tier

Standard CPI catalogue · top-offender template · transition factory · 2 pilot accounts

Q3–Q4 · 6–12 months
Reuse & attach

Observability pack · SPOG patterns · automation library · tier step-up renewals

Year 2
Autonomize scale

Closed-loop agents · policy packs · full AO SKU merge (Case Study 2)

Fund (high ROI)

Priority investments

  • Global rate card + margin model — stops bleeding immediately
  • CPI / SLA catalogue (reuse H3G 75-KPI pattern) — one language for Sales & Delivery
  • Sales enablement — tier pitch, discovery kit, executive demo (SPOG + CPI board)
  • Transition pricing module — protects Y1 GM on every new logo
  • 2 regional pilots (e.g. UK + LATAM) — prove reposition before global mandate
Deprioritize / stop

Explicitly not now

  • Custom SIEM/observability builds per account — use standard pack; charge for exceptions
  • Greenfield tool R&D without reuse thesis — portfolio funds AO platform, not AMS one-offs
  • Headcount-heavy PoCs sold below floor GM to “win logo”
  • Regional bespoke offerings — breaks global rate card; route through adapter packs
  • Marketing rebrand without sales kit — story without Deal Desk tools = no conversion
ROI logic: Q1 investments are mostly product/GTM labor — low capex, immediate guardrail effect. Q2–Q4 reuse delivery IP already proven in H3G/TEF accounts — amortize across AMS base. Year-2 autonomy spend links to Case Study 2 reusable AO offering — avoid duplicate tooling tax.
Deliverable 5 · Cross-functional alignment

Who does what — Sales, Marketing, Finance, Regions

Repositioning is a portfolio program, not a product deck in isolation. Each function has a named outcome, artifact, and cadence.

Sales & Bid / Deal Desk

  • Outcome: ≥80% of new AMS bids use tier SKU + rate card by Q2
  • Artifacts: discovery kit · ROI calc · margin waterfall · approval matrix
  • Cadence: weekly deal review on reposition pilots; win/loss tagged to tier
  • Lead: Global Sales Enablement + Regional Product Manager (matrix)

Marketing & Portfolio

  • Outcome: one external narrative — “Application Operations → Autonomy”
  • Artifacts: tier one-pagers · customer proof stories (H3G/TEF anonymized) · analyst brief
  • Cadence: monthly message test with Sales; retire “FTE tower” language from collaterals
  • Lead: Services Portfolio Marketing + SPM owner

Finance — P&L, rev-rec, compliance

  • Outcome: tier pricing compliant with revenue recognition; GM floors in ERP bid model
  • Artifacts: rev-rec memo per tier (platform fee vs outcome/milestone) · penalty parking lot accounting
  • Cadence: Deal Desk sign-off on any outcome/milestone element before customer proposal
  • Lead: Regional Finance BP + Global Services Controller

Regional enablement & Delivery

  • Outcome: pilot regions deliver to CPI catalogue; transition factory repeatable
  • Artifacts: KT/shadow playbook · SDM CPI board · escalation path to Product
  • Cadence: bi-weekly pilot steering; QBR tier step-up proposals on live accounts
  • Lead: Regional Delivery Head + AO CoE (reuse library)

Program governance

  • Steering forum: SPM (chair) · Sales VP · Marketing · Finance · 2 Regional MDs — monthly
  • Decision rights: SPM owns SKU/tier; Finance owns rev-rec; Sales owns pipeline targets; Delivery owns CPI attainment
  • Change management: train-the-trainer in pilot regions; resist “shadow rate cards” via Deal Desk audit
  • Link to Case Study 2: Autonomize tier is the commercial on-ramp to the reusable AO offering — shared KPI catalogue and reuse library
Deliverable 6 · Success metrics & feedback loop

How we know reposition worked — and how we keep refining

Metrics split into leading (commercial motion) and lagging (P&L outcomes). A quarterly pricing council adjusts tiers, floors, and packs based on field data — not annual slide refresh.

Metric Baseline (today) 12-month target Owner
Net sales growth (AMS) Flat (~0%) +8–12% (tier land + attach) Sales VP
Gross margin % −4–6 pts vs 2yr ago Recover 3–4 pts; within 2 pts of portfolio target Finance / SPM
Bids using tier SKU + rate card <20% (ad hoc FTE) ≥80% of qualified pipeline Deal Desk
Regional price variance (like-for-like) >25% <15% SPM
Win rate — “value/TCO” cited <20% ≥40% of wins Marketing / Sales
Tier step-up / attach at renewal N/A (no tiers) ≥25% of renewals upsell Optimize+ Regional Delivery
Delivery NPS (hold) Strong ≥ baseline — no trade-down for margin Delivery

Feedback loop — quarterly pricing council

  • Review win/loss tagged by tier, discount depth, competitor
  • Adjust rate card index ±3% max per cycle
  • Add/retire modular packs based on attach data
  • Escalate systematic delivery gaps to AO CoE

Field signals — monthly

  • Deal Desk override log → guardrail patches
  • Scope CR volume per account → packaging fix
  • Sales survey: “what blocked the bid?”
  • Pilot CPI attainment → tier credibility proof

Annual portfolio review

  • Sunset test re-run — GM trajectory vs 18-month rule
  • Merge Autonomize tier into AO SKU (Case Study 2)
  • Investor narrative: AMS as land, AO as expand
  • Retire legacy FTE-only contracts on renewal
